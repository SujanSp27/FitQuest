## 🎨 **Design System**

### **Color Palette:**
- **Primary**: #00C2FF (Cyan)
- **Secondary**: #14F1C5 (Mint)
- **Accent**: #00A3B8 (Teal)
- **Background**: #0A0E11 to #1a2332 (Dark gradients)
- **Text**: #FFFFFF, #B8EFFF, #7ED7E8 (Light variants)

### **Typography:**
- **Title**: 18-22px, Bold, White
- **Subtitle**: 12-13px, Medium, Light Blue
- **Tags**: 11-12px, Semi-bold, Colored
- **Button**: 14-16px, Bold, Dark (on light background)

### **Spacing:**
- **Card Height**: 420-460px (responsive)
- **Border Radius**: 24px (modern rounded corners)
- **Padding**: 16-24px (consistent spacing)
- **Gaps**: 8-16px (proper element separation)
